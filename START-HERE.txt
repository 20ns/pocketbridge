PocketBridge is built and running on this Mac.

Open http://127.0.0.1:8787 for the Mac client. The service starts at login and
keeps the Mac awake on AC power with the lid open. PocketBridge is already
registered as a project; use Add folder to register your other project paths.

ONE-TIME PHONE SETUP
1. Open Tailscale on the Mac, sign in and approve its macOS VPN/network extension.
   Install Tailscale on the S25 from Google Play and sign in to the same account.
2. Double-click mac/launcher/Setup private connection.command in this folder.
   It configures private HTTPS Serve, saves the address and restarts PocketBridge.
   If it prints a Tailscale HTTPS enablement link, complete that step and rerun it.
3. Sign into GitHub on your phone and download the latest PocketBridge APK from
   https://github.com/20ns/pocketbridge/releases/latest . Install it over the old app.
   The local releases folder also contains PocketBridge-0.3.0.apk.
   Alternatively, open the private address from step 2 followed by /PocketBridge.apk
   in Chrome on your phone. Android will ask for the usual APK installation approval.
4. In the Mac client choose Connect phone, then Create pairing code.
5. On the phone enter the private address and one-time code in PocketBridge.
   Or scan the QR with the phone camera to prefill them, then tap Connect to Mac.

After pairing, open the app, choose a project and make a chat. Bypass permissions
is the default; Auto is also available. The mode is remembered per chat. Send a
prompt, read streamed text/activity, answer questions and use Stop when needed.
The Mac continues a turn while the app is closed. Reopening catches up.

Pairing stays saved across app/service restarts. Keep Tailscale connected on both
devices. Claude sign-in and Tailscale device keys can independently require renewal;
disable key expiry for your own devices in Tailscale if desired.

Only chats started through PocketBridge appear. Use its Mac client to continue a
phone chat on the Mac. Existing Claude terminal/desktop sessions are separate.
Stop does not undo completed edits. Ready means the Claude turn has ended;
PocketBridge cleans up tools left running in that turn. Concurrent
manual file edits have the usual filesystem conflict risk.

The app runs the official, unmodified Claude Code CLI. It keeps Claude login on
the Mac and never extracts subscription tokens or implements a substitute login.
Provider usage limits and subscription rules still apply. This is a personal CLI
controller, not an Anthropic-approved product or a guarantee against future changes.
Official reference: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance

To stop startup, use mac/launcher/Remove startup.command. This keeps your chats.
