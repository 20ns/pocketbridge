One-time setup

  1. Sign in to Tailscale on your Mac and phone.
  2. Open Setup private connection.command in mac/launcher.
  3. The helper saves your private address. Follow its restart instructions before pairing.
  4. Create a pairing code here, then scan it on your phone.

Use private Serve. Do not enable Funnel. Your app connection persists; Claude may still ask you to sign in again.
