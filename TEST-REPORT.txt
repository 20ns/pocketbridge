DISTRIBUTION AND LIMITS
Public source and signed APKs: https://github.com/20ns/pocketbridge
Release assets include SHA256. Updates retain the original persistent certificate
and package name. Credentials, databases, signing keys and private captures remain
ignored. The APK is non-debuggable and intended for personal sideloading.

Keep the Mac logged in, awake, plugged in and lid open. Android update installation
requires the system's confirmation. Real Samsung roaming/performance and physical
0.4 installation still need the user's phone trial. Mac logout/FileVault, closed
lid and battery-only sleep are outside the agreed operating setup. Markdown is a
practical subset, not a complete CommonMark engine. Stop cannot undo edits or stop
explicitly detached process groups. Questions have a 24-hour waiting ceiling.
Provider login, model availability, subscription limits and Tailscale renewal are
independent of saved PocketBridge pairing; no account-ban guarantee is claimed.
